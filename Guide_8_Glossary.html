M1S SOP HUB · GLOSSARY

Glossary of Key Terms

Look up the words used across our trackers, reports and guides.

OwnerSaul Braha
Who it's forEveryone
WhenWhen a word is new to you

TermWhat it means
Agreed termsWhen and how the customer must pay. They go on each customer invoice. See Customer Invoice.
ATSAvailable to sell. See Inventory Report.
ATS reportThe weekly report from the ERP, showing stock and what's available to sell. The Shalam Tool reads it.
BackorderThe part of a PO that hasn't shipped. It moves to the PO's next backorder number (-1, -2 and so on), with its own quantity and Factory CRD. The PO Book tags it BO.
BannerThe store chain a PO is for.
Batch #The manufacturing or lot number. Batch # Checked on the PO Status Tracker is one of the checks before goods can be booked.
BFBottle Find: bottle and component sourcing. This Dev Tracker tab came off on Nov 2, 2026. Nothing new can be added there.
BookedThe goods are placed under a round on the Booking tab of the Container Tracking Sheet. Lines not booked yet sit under a planning section. See Container Planning & Logistics.
BSBenchmark Sample files and their follow-up. A tab on the Dev Tracker. See Development & Sample Tracking.
BufferIn the PO Book, the days between Factory CRD and Cust. Start. On an LA PO, the LA transit offset comes off too, 30 days unless Settings says otherwise. See Production & PO Tracking.
BuyThe POs one customer sent together. PO Recap saves and checks them as one buy. See Compliance & Routing.
CAD #A style number from the Quote Tracker's own sequence. Tick Auto-assign CAD # in the + New Quote popup to get the next one.
Cancel dateThe end of a customer's ship window. Goods that reach our warehouse after it count as late in Late & Short.
Carton marksThe markings on master cartons. Confirmed before shipping.
Case packThe number of units in a master carton.
CBMCubic meters: the volume of the cargo.
China PickupAn Order Type on the PO Status Tracker. The goods are already sold and ship to the customer from China, so they're never available to sell.
ChipA small rounded label on a tool's screen. At the top of the PO Book and the Shalam Tool, each chip names a file or list the tool reads. Click it to open it. A chip with a PO number, above the PO Book's table, is a filter: click it to show everything again.
Commercial invoice (C.I.)A shipping document. It's tracked with the packing list, in the P.L/ C.I. column on the Container Tracking Sheet.
Container Tracking SheetThe Excel workbook that follows each round through its Booking, Loaded, Open and Complete tabs. See Container Planning & Logistics.
CRDCargo Ready Date: when goods are ready to ship. Factory CRD is the date the factory gives. Delayed CRD is for a Factory CRD later than expected.
Customer PO#The customer's own number for its order. Late & Short calls it Cust PO.
DCDistribution center. A distro says how many units go to each one.
DeductionAn amount the customer took off when paying an invoice. See Customer Invoice.
Dev PLMOur web tool for samples and orders. The Dev Tracker, the Order Tracker and Reports are in it.
Dev TrackerThe screen in Dev PLM that follows each style and sample, from artwork to approval and ingredients. See Development & Sample Tracking.
DistroDistribution and allocation detail: the customer's routing and distribution instructions for one PO. It says how many units go to each DC. PO Recap checks TJX distros against their POs. Distro Sent is a column on the PO Status Tracker.
DODelivery order. It goes to the trucker. DO Sent to trucker on the Container Tracking Sheet has the date.
DrayageTrucking from the port to the warehouse. The drayage report gives pickup and delivery dates.
ERPThe company's stock and order system.
ETAEstimated Time of Arrival at destination. In Late & Short, it's the date at the port.
ETDEstimated Time of Departure: when the goods leave.
Exclusive copyA second order the Order Tracker makes for a row with a number in Exclusive Stock Qty, under the customer name plus Exclusive. See Order Placement.
FDAThe US Food and Drug Administration. FDA Released on the Container Tracking Sheet is for FDA's release.
FFFragrance Find: fragrance sourcing. This Dev Tracker tab came off on Nov 2, 2026. Nothing new can be added there.
FOBFree On Board: the factory price before freight and duty. PO Recap also checks the FOB point on a customer's PO against that customer's rule.
ForwarderThe company moving the shipment. Its report gives each container's status.
Fragrance LibraryOur tool for factory fragrance samplers, and the scents picked for each style. Its tile is on the Tools Hub. See Add a scent.
HKThe Hong Kong office. It records inspection results on the Inspection Tracker.
Import POOur PO with the factory, as the ERP and Late & Short call it.
INCIInternational Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients: the standard names for cosmetic ingredients. In Dev PLM, the INCI ingredient list on Artwork holds a style's ingredients, one list per Style #. Artwork is in Preview, so only admins see it today.
IncomingGoods in China, on the water or at port. See Inventory Report.
Inspection TrackerThe Excel workbook that logs every QC inspection, by PO and style. See Inspection.
KanbanThe Dev Tracker's card view, with one column for each PP Sample Status. See Use the Kanban.
LALos Angeles. In the PO Book, LA is a PO Type: any PO that isn't China Pickup.
LandedThe goods have arrived. A round counts as Landed once it's on the Complete tab of the Container Tracking Sheet. The Shalam Tool also counts it as Landed when drayage shows an actual delivery date. Late & Short calls that Delivered.
Landed costThe cost per unit after freight, duty, warehouse and allowances.
LateIn Late & Short: the goods reach our warehouse after the cancel date.
Late & ShortThe main daily view of orders at risk: what's late, short or tight. See Order Tracking.
LFDLast free day: the last day a container can sit at port with no extra charge.
LineOne style on one PO or customer order.
LiveIn Dev PLM, a tool that opens for everyone with rights on it. See Check the tool is Live.
M1S StockAn Order Type on the PO Status Tracker, also written under Customer. It has no outside customer, so Customer PO# is blank in the PO Book.
Master Sheet LiveThe Excel workbook with each PO's lines, quantities and prices. A row whose Product name starts with Round splits it: lines above the last Round count as shipped, and lines below it are open. The PO Book's MSL only tag means a line is on this sheet but not on the PO Status Tracker.
Master/InnerPack sizes, shown as two numbers such as 48/12. The first is the case pack. The second is the inner pack. The M1S order form writes it Master/ Inner, and the Order Tracker M/I.
Microsoft EntraMicrosoft's admin site for accounts and app access. See New Hire Onboarding.
MoCRAModernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act: the US law on cosmetic products, including listing them with the FDA.
MOQMinimum order quantity. On a quote sheet, the factory fills it in.
NYCThe New York office. It sets up each inspection on the Inspection Tracker.
Order CutA column on the M1S order form. One file can make several orders, one for each customer and each value in this column. See Order Placement.
Order Submission ToolThe old name for what is now the Order Tracker. Saul to confirm.
Order TrackerThe screen in Dev PLM that holds confirmed orders and their items. See Order Placement.
Packing list (P.L)The list of what's packed in a shipment. It's tracked in the P.L/ C.I. column on the Container Tracking Sheet. Packing list sent to WH has the date it went to the warehouse.
Past cancelIn Late & Short: the cancel date has passed and the line is still open.
PDQPoint-of-purchase display, with its own die lines (the cutting outline for the display) and artwork. PDQ pricing in the Quote Directory asks each factory for two prices: with PDQ and without.
PIProforma Invoice: the factory's first version of the bill for a PO, listing the goods and prices. It's signed before production. PI Status on the PO Status Tracker says where it stands, such as Signed or Price Issue. See Factory Invoice.
POPurchase order. In the PO Book, PO # is our order with the factory. The customer's own number is the Customer PO#.
PO BookShows what's on order with the factories, and what has shipped from them. See Production & PO Tracking.
PO RecapReads a customer's POs and flags what needs a look. For TJX, it also checks them against the distros. For Burlington, it reads POs and ticketing, but not routing or distro files. See Compliance & Routing.
PO Status TrackerThe Excel sheet Production keeps up to date for each PO, with one row per style. It opens from a chip at the top of the PO Book. See Production & PO Tracking.
PPSPre-Production Sample: final approval before mass production. The Dev Tracker shows where it stands under PP Sample Status.
PrepackBurlington's word for the inner pack, the smaller pack inside a carton. See Check a Burlington buy.
PreviewIn Dev PLM, a tool only admins can open so far. It has a PREVIEW tag in the side menu. See Check the tool is Live.
Pull from ChinaOur goods in China that aren't sold yet, by the date a buyer can have them. See Inventory Report.
QCQuality control. QC inspections are logged on the Inspection Tracker.
Quote DirectoryWhere quote sheets are made, sent to factories and compared. See Quoting Out to Factories.
Quote sheetThe list of styles sent to factories for prices. Its name looks like N26Q06: November 2026, quote 6. See Quoting Out to Factories.
Quote TrackerThe one log of new items, followed until an order is placed. See Item Intake & Quoting.
Recently deletedThe Dev PLM page that keeps deleted rows for 90 days. Only admins can open it. See Bring back something deleted.
ReinspectionAn inspection done again after a FAIL. It goes on a new row, with Reinspection PO and the PO number under PO#.
Round #The number of a round. A round is one shipment, such as Round 44. Goods that ship together are consolidated under it. It can have more than one container.
Sample WarehouseOur tool for sample boxes only, not containers. See Use Sample Warehouse for sample boxes.
SCPPS Comments files sent to factories. A tab on the Dev Tracker. See Development & Sample Tracking.
Shalam SalesThe page for salespeople. It shows the saved On hand ATS and Pull from China files, with photos. See Offer goods from Shalam Sales.
Shalam ToolShows what we can sell, where the goods are, and when they can be pulled from China. See Inventory Report.
Ship windowThe customer's dates for an order, from the start ship date to the cancel date. Late & Short calls it Window. Notes on Master Sheet Live write it as SW, as in SW 12/2-12/10.
ShippedIn the PO Book: the goods have left the factory. They may not have reached our warehouse or the customer yet. See Order Tracking.
ShortIn Late & Short: the ERP shows units short on a line.
Short paymentThe customer paid less than the invoice. It's about money, not units. See Customer Invoice.
SOP HubThis process library: the workflow board that opens these guides.
SRSample Request files sent to factories. A tab on the Dev Tracker. Its numbers look like O26SR19: October 2026, SR, number 19. Older numbers may say SF.
SRDSample Ready Date. In the Dev Tracker, a grey, slanted Estimated SRD is only an estimate.
Style #The code for one item, such as SAMPLE-1001 or MAY26Q08-01-A. Styles ending in -A or -B are pricing variants of the same item.
Style listThe list of styles: UPCs, descriptions, categories and brands. Exports and other tools read it.
SupplierThe factory. The PO Book and the Dev Tracker use this word.
TelexRelease of the goods without the original bill of lading, the carrier's shipping document.
TicketsRetail price tickets or labels.
TightIn Late & Short: the goods reach our warehouse inside the ship window, but 5 days or less before the cancel date.
TJX TrackerHolds the weekly TJX reports, and shows TJX's open orders and reorders. See Order Tracking.
Tools HubThe page of links to our tools. You see a card for each tool ticked for you. See Give access in the Tools Hub.
UPCA product's barcode number, 12 or 13 digits.
Users & permissionsThe Dev PLM page where admins add people and set their rights. See Give access in Dev PLM.
WeTransferThe site for sending big files. See Send artwork by hand.
WHWarehouse. In WH on the ATS report is the physical stock.
